wiggly path = distance straight arrow = displacement
Distance is a scalar — it only ever grows, counting every metre Pip walks no matter the direction. Displacement is a vector — final spot minus starting spot, so it can be positive, negative, or even zero! Try walking Pip three steps right, then three back: distance says 6 m, but displacement pops right back to 0.
