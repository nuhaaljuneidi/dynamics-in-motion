ACTIVITY 05 · RECTILINEAR MOTION WITH CONSTANT ACCELERATION

Problem from Chapter 13: A ball is thrown straight upward from a point 1.2 m above the ground. After 3 s, the ball returns to the ground. Determine the maximum height reached. Use g = 9.81 m/s².

Motion model

Position1.20 m
Velocity14.32 m/s
Acceleration−9.81 m/s²

Guided solution

1

Establish signs and states

Choose upward as positive. Therefore gravity is negative: a = −9.81 m/s².

t₀=0, s₀=1.2 m, v₀=?
t₂=3 s, s₂=0

2

Find the unknown initial velocity

s₂=s₀+v₀t+½at²
0=1.2+v₀(3)+½(−9.81)(3²)

m/s

3

Recognize the top condition

At maximum height the ball changes direction, so its instantaneous velocity is:

m/s

4

Find maximum height without time

v₁²=v₀²+2a(s₁−s₀)
0=(14.32)²+2(−9.81)(s₁−1.2)

m

Final result

Maximum height ≈ 11.6 m above the ground

The ball reaches the top at approximately t=v₀/g≈1.46 s, then falls to the ground at 3.00 s.

Why solve for v₀ first?

The total flight time and final position are known, but the launch velocity is not. The position-time equation connects those known values directly.

Why is velocity zero at the top?

The ball pauses for an instant while changing from upward to downward motion. Acceleration is still −9.81 m/s² there; only velocity is zero.
