N-T vs. Polar

ACTIVITY 06

A car takes an S-curve on-ramp at a constant 15 m/s — one bend, then a tighter one curving the other way (idealized as two circular arcs sharing a common tangent). The car has exactly one real velocity and one real acceleration. n-t and polar are just two different bases for writing those same two vectors: v = v_t e_t = ṙe_r + rθ̇e_θ and a = a_t e_t + a_n e_n = a_r e_r + a_θe_θ. The components look different because the axes are different — not because the motion changed.
