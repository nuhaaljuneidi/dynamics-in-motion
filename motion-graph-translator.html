Interpreting the Graphs

Position is where the car is. Its slope at any moment is the velocity. The velocity graph's slope is the acceleration. Viewed in reverse, acceleration describes how quickly velocity changes, while the signed area under velocity gives displacement. Total distance is the area under speed, |v|. Drag a point to observe how the three graphs remain synchronized.
